Chapter 01 · The beginning

For Rahma.

Some stories are not written in a single day. They are made from little moments, quiet smiles, unexpected memories, and the hope that tomorrow will be gentle.

Chapter 02 · A smile01 / 11

Some smiles make a room feel warmer.

May you always have reasons to smile that are deeper than a photograph — reasons that stay with you when the picture is gone.

Chapter 03 · A quiet moment02 / 11

May peace find you before you have to ask for it.

For the days when your thoughts become too loud: may you find a corner of calm, a gentle breath, and the strength to leave tomorrow for tomorrow.

Chapter 04 · A gentle light03 / 11

There is beauty in gentleness.

May your kindness never become something you feel you have to hide. May it be protected, respected, and returned to you in ways you never expected.

Chapter 05 · A different side04 / 11

Keep the little version of yourself that knows how to play.

Life can be serious enough. May you always keep room for laughter, silly moments, unexpected jokes, and the freedom to simply be yourself.

Chapter 06 · A playful moment05 / 11

May your laughter remain yours.

Not every beautiful moment needs a grand explanation. Some are precious simply because they made you laugh.

Chapter 07 · A familiar smile06 / 11

May good days become ordinary for you.

May you wake up without fear, learn without pressure, laugh without pretending, and sleep with a heart that feels safe.

Chapter 08 · A quiet gaze07 / 11

Some moments say nothing — and still say enough.

May you never underestimate the value of the quiet parts of your life: rest, reflection, learning, prayer, and simply breathing.

Chapter 09 · A bright memory08 / 11

May your future hold more light than you can imagine.

May every new chapter bring knowledge, dignity, sincere companionship, and opportunities that make you grateful you kept going.

Chapter 10 · A playful collection09 / 11

Keep making memories worth keeping.

One day, the small photographs become the large memories. Keep the moments that make your heart feel alive.

Chapter 11 · A soft morning10 / 11

May Allah make your path clear.

May knowledge benefit you, may your efforts be accepted, and may every step you take bring you closer to what is good for you.

Chapter 12 · The last memory11 / 11

And may there always be another reason to smile.

This is only a page, not the whole story. The best parts of life are still unwritten.

Chapter 13 · Wishes

Choose a little prayer.

Chapter 14 · A night prayer
“May the nights you spend overthinking become fewer, and the mornings you wake up grateful become many.”

May Allah guard your heart, ease what you cannot explain, increase you in beneficial knowledge, protect your family, and guide you toward every door that contains goodness.

Chapter 15 · A letter

There is something I wanted you to know.

Not everything meaningful needs a perfect sentence. Sometimes the most honest thing is simply this: I hope life is gentle with you, I hope you never forget your worth, and I hope Allah writes beautiful things for you in places you cannot yet see.

Chapter 16 · The last page

May what is written for you be beautiful.

May Allah place sakinah in your heart, barakah in your time, light in your decisions, mercy in your home, and goodness in the people who surround you. May He protect you from what is harmful, guide you toward what is beneficial, and make your future better than your fears.

Ameen. ♥

A little prayer

A private letter

Dear Rahma,

Some people become important quietly. Not because of grand moments, but because of the small things: a conversation, a laugh, a kind word, a moment remembered long after it happened.

I hope you never forget that your worth is not measured by how productive you are, how quickly you reply, how perfect you feel, or how well you carry every difficulty. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to grow slowly. You are allowed to become.

And above all, I pray that Allah guides your path with wisdom and mercy. May He protect your heart, bless your learning, surround you with people who treat you gently, and write for you a future filled with halal happiness, peace and barakah.

May your next chapter be beautiful.
With sincere good wishes ♥
