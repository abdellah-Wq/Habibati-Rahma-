01 · BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

I know these days
have been a lot.

So I will not ask you to smile, explain everything, or carry one more thing for someone else. For a few minutes, let this place ask nothing from you.

Take a slow breath. Drink some water. Sleep if you can. Leave tomorrow's problems for tomorrow.
You do not have to carry everything in one day.
02 · MOMENTS I WANT TO KEEP

Your smile.Some smiles do not need an explanation.

03 · A QUIET PROMISE

When you are tired,
I do not need a hero.

I want you to be gentle with yourself. Being exhausted is not a failure. Needing space is not a failure. Resting is not a failure.

If I can choose one thing to be for you, I want it to be peace, not pressure — a safe corner, not another demand.
RAHMA
04 · OPEN WHEN YOU NEED IT

Little letters
for heavy days.

When you're tired

You do not have to fix everything tonight. Let some things wait. Your heart and body deserve a quiet moment.

When everything feels heavy

Step away from the noise for a while. Breathe. Make du'a. A difficult feeling does not have to become a permanent day.

When you smile

This time, we do not need to rescue the day. We simply celebrate that your smile appeared.

06 · THE LAST THING

Rahma,
remember this.

I ask Allah to replace your tiredness with sakinah, your worry with ease, and your heavy moments with relief. May He fill your heart with peace and give you days that feel lighter than these.

You do not have to be okay every minute. Just be gentle with yourself.
